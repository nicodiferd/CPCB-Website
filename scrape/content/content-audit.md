## Tone and Voice

### Brand Voice Characteristics

**Professional:**
- Represents Cal Poly and NCBA
- Clear, correct grammar and spelling
- Respectful of opponents and league

**Competitive:**
- Highlights achievements
- Emphasizes winning culture
- Celebrates success

**Welcoming:**
- Invites new players
- Engages fans and supporters
- Builds community

**Passionate:**
- Shows love for baseball
- Demonstrates school spirit
- Conveys team pride

### Voice by Content Type

**Informational:** Straightforward, factual, clear

**Recruitment:** Enthusiastic, inviting, detailed

**Game Updates:** Energetic, immediate, exciting

**News/Blog:** Engaging, storytelling, celebratory

**Social Media:** Casual, energetic, visual, hashtag-friendly

---

## Writing Style Guidelines

### Grammar and Mechanics
- AP Style (standard for sports)
- Consistent terminology
- Active voice preferred
- Present tense for live updates
- Past tense for recaps

### Formatting
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Bullet points for lists
- Bold for emphasis (sparingly)
- Headers to break up long content
- White space for readability

### Numbers and Stats
- Numerals for all game stats
- Spell out one through nine in general text
- Use standard baseball abbreviations (W, L, ERA, RBI, etc.)

## Accessibility Content Guidelines

### Text Accessibility
- Minimum 16px font size for body
- Adequate line spacing (1.5+)
- High contrast text/background
- No text in images (or alt text provided)
- Plain language, avoid jargon

### Media Accessibility
- Alt text for all images
- Captions for videos
- Transcripts for audio
- Descriptive link text (not "click here")
