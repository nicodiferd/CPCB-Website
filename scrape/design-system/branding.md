# CPCB Mustangs Website - Branding and Visual Identity

## Overview
The CPCB (Cal Poly Club Baseball) Mustangs website branding follows Cal Poly Athletics visual identity guidelines while maintaining a distinct club baseball identity. The branding emphasizes team spirit, collegiate athletics, and the Mustang mascot.

---

## Brand Identity

### Team Name
- **Full Name:** Cal Poly Club Baseball (CPCB)
- **Short Name:** CP Club Baseball
- **Nickname:** Mustangs
- **Common Abbreviations:** CPCB, CP Baseball

### Tagline/Mission (Inferred)
- Competitive club baseball team
- Member of National Club Baseball Association (NCBA)
- Division 1 Southern Pacific West Conference
- Achievements: "2x SoPac Regional Champs, 8x SoPac West Division Champs"

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
Based on Cal Poly Athletics branding guidelines:

**Description:**
- Features Cal Poly Mustang mascot
- Bold, athletic design
- Green and gold color scheme
- Dynamic, forward-facing mustang

**Logo Variations:**
1. **Full Logo:** Mustang + "Cal Poly" wordmark
2. **Icon Only:** Mustang head/mascot without text
3. **Wordmark Only:** "Cal Poly" or "Mustangs" text treatment

**Colors Used:**
- Cal Poly Green (#003831)
- Vegas Gold (#FFE395)
- Copper Gold (#B38F4F)
- Black (#000000)
- White (#FFFFFF)

### Logo Placement
- **Header:** Top left corner (standard web convention)
- **Size:** Approximately 40px-60px height on desktop
- **Mobile:** May scale down or show icon-only version
- **Footer:** May include smaller version or wordmark

### Usage Guidelines
Following Cal Poly Athletics sub-brand rules:
- Logo reserved for athletics department use
- Should not replace primary Cal Poly institutional logo
- Represents the proud tradition of Mustangs sports teams
- Maintains consistency with official athletics branding
